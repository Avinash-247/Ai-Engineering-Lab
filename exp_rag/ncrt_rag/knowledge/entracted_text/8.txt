More Expressions and Their Terms
Example 7: Amu, Charan, Madhu, and John went to a hotel and ordered 
four dosas. Each dosa cost ₹23, and they wish to thank the waiter by 
tipping ₹5. Write an expression describing the total cost.
Cost of 4 dosas = 4 × 23           
Can the total amount with tip be written as 4 × 23 + 5? Evaluating it, 
we get
4 × 23 925 5+ +4 × 23 + 5 = 97= =
Thus, 4 × 23 + 5 is a correct way of writing the expression.
