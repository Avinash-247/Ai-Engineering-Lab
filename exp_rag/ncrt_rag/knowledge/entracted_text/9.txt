Example 9:  Raghu bought 100 kg of rice from the wholesale market 
and packed them into 2 kg packets. He already had four 2 kg packets. 
Write an expression for the number of 2 kg packets of rice he has now 
and identify the terms.  
He had 4 packets. The number of new 2 kg packets of rice is 100 ÷ 2, 
which we also write as 
100
2 . 
The number of 2 kg packets he has now is 4 + 100

4 100

Example 10: Kannan has to pay ₹432 to a shopkeeper using coins of ₹1 
and ₹5, and notes of ₹10, ₹20, ₹50 and ₹100. How can he do it? 
There is more than one possibility. For example, 
432 = 4 × 100 + 1 × 20 + 1 × 10 + 2 × 1
Meaning: 4 notes of ₹100, 1 note of ₹20, 1 note of ₹10 and 2 notes of ₹1
432 = 8 × 50 + 1 × 10 + 4 × 5 + 2 × 1 
Meaning: 8 notes of ₹50, 1 note of ₹10, 4 notes of ₹5 and 2 notes of ₹1
Identify the terms in the two expressions above.
Can you think of some more ways of giving  ₹432 to someone?
