5. Add brackets at appropriate places in the expressions such that they 
lead to the values indicated. 
(a) 34 – 9 + 12 = 13 
(b) 56 – 14 – 8 = 34 
(c) – 22 – 12 – 10 + 22 = – 22 
Ans:  
(a) 34 – (9 + 12) = 34 – 21 = 13 
(b) (56 – 14) – 8 = 42 – 8 = 34 
(c) – 22 – (12 + 10) + 22 = – 22 – 22 + 22 = – 22
