Q.7.   Puzzle: I am an acute angle. If you double my measure, you get an acute angle. If 
you triple my measure, you will get an acute angle again. If you quadruple (four 
times) my measure, you will get an acute angle yet again! But if you multiply my 
measure by 5, you will get an obtuse angle measure. What are the possibilities for 
my measure? 
Ans.   The acute angle can be 19º, 20º, 21º & 22º.
