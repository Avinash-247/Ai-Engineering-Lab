# 🌌 The Lantern in the Forest

## Introduction
Long ago, in a quiet village surrounded by dense forests, people whispered about a glowing lantern that appeared only on moonless nights.

## The Discovery
- **Arjun**, a curious boy, decided to follow the light.
- He carried only:
  - A small bag of food
  - His grandfather’s compass
  - A brave heart

## The Journey
As he walked deeper:
1. The trees grew taller.
2. The silence became heavier.
3. The lantern flickered brighter.

Arjun realized the lantern wasn’t hanging from a branch—it floated, guiding him.

## The Secret
The lantern led him to a hidden clearing where an **ancient oak tree** stood.  
Carved into its trunk were words: *“Light guides those who seek truth.”*

## Conclusion
Arjun returned to the village, not with treasure, but with wisdom:  
> Sometimes, the journey itself is the gift, and the light we chase is already within us.
